# 2026-08-21 — Section 338 Canada tariffs: entry date, not ship date

**Angle:** U.S. Section 338 50% duties on covered Canadian goods (dairy, alcoholic beverages, motor vehicles) take effect 12:01 a.m. ET August 22 after a three-day pause. First historical use of Section 338. Duty keyed to entry/warehouse withdrawal — not ship date; USMCA preference does not exempt covered lines; bonded inventory still exposed. Distinct from Aug 11 Section 301 compliance-stack theme.
