# 2026-08-11 — Section 301 tariff calendar remakes peak season

**Angle:** USTR Section 301 forced-labor tariffs (10%–12.5%) on ~60 economies took effect July 24, covering ~99.4% of U.S. imports. July TEUs frontloaded ahead of the deadline (China year-high); NRF/Hackett projects August–November hangover. Peak season now tracks Federal Register dates more than holiday demand. Compliance stack (origin, Chapter 99, FTA carve-outs, refund uncertainty from 25-state suit) is the real operational risk.
