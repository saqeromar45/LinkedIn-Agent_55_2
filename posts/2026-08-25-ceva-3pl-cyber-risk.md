## LinkedIn copy

Your warehouse didn’t go down. Your 3PL’s systems did — and so did your customer data.

In late July, a cyber intrusion hit Ceva Logistics’ European contract logistics network. Eight warehouses stopped shipping. Downstream brands — from Bol and De Bijenkorf to Valve’s Steam hardware — faced order delays, cancellations, and customer notifications after names, addresses, phones, and order details were exposed in the partner’s systems. Dutch authorities logged breach reports from about ten organizations tied to a single 3PL incident.

This is the new shape of supply-chain risk: operational disruption and privacy exposure travel together through shared fulfillment platforms. Resilience is no longer only alternate carriers and safety stock. It is vendor cyber posture, data-retention limits, failover playbooks, and clear notification chains when a partner’s WMS goes dark.

If your fulfillment still assumes the 3PL’s uptime equals your uptime — what cyber contingency are you testing before peak season?
