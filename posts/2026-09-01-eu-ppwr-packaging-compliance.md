# 2026-09-01 — EU PPWR packaging compliance

**Angle:** EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR, Reg. 2025/40) entered application on 12 August 2026. Harmonised design/DoC rules vs still-national EPR registration across 27 member states; transport packaging + 3PL unpacking/repacking can shift “producer” status. Distinct from Aug 31 CBAM theme (carbon certificates vs packaging EPR/DoC).

**Image:** `assets/eu-ppwr-packaging-compliance.png`

**Short copy:**
```
Your carton just became a customs-adjacent compliance file.

Since August 12, the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation has applied across the Single Market. Design rules and Declarations of Conformity are now EU-wide — but Extended Producer Responsibility still runs through 27 national registers. Brands shipping into multiple member states need per-country registration, packaging data by material and weight, and eco-modulated fees. Non-EU sellers often need an authorised representative. Marketplaces are already treating missing EPR numbers as a listing risk.

For logistics teams, the operational catch is transport packaging and fulfilment. When a 3PL receives third-country goods and unpacks or repacks them, that operator can become the “producer” for packaging that becomes waste in the Union — even without owning the goods. Empty-space limits, reuse targets, and recycled-content mandates still phase in later, but the documentation and EPR layer is live now.

If your EU playbook still treats packaging as a cost-of-goods detail — who owns the EPR registration and Declaration of Conformity for every carton you move?
```
