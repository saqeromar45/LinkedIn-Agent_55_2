Ports reopen. The backlog doesn’t.

Typhoon Dolphin — the third and strongest storm to hit China’s east coast in five weeks — forced multi-day closures at Shanghai and Ningbo. Linerlytica tracked more than 2.4 million TEUs of container capacity stranded across North Asia. Inland drayage and air export space out of east China are still clearing weekend cancellations. Weather is no longer a one-off delay — it’s a recurring capacity constraint on the world’s busiest export corridor.
